Matching jobs

Grad Zagreb · open to third-country nationals

Method

Method

Subset of public HZZ Burza rada listings. Not the full catalogue. Not a work-permit or sponsorship guarantee.

Inclusion

A listing appears only if all of the following hold:

  1. County. Grad Zagreb radio value 4. Occupation categories are walked one by one; “Svi poslovi” is capped at 300 rows.
  2. Location. location_score > 0 (2 = city centre, 1 = Zagreb, 0 = drop).
  3. Foreigner score. Weighted phrases in title + employer + detail. Shown only if score ≥ FOREIGN_SCORE_THRESHOLD (2). Details below.

Other counties are not scraped. Inspected non-matches are not shown.

Foreigner score

This is the only filter beyond scraping Grad Zagreb HZZ ads. It is a weighted substring score over public listing copy. It is not a work-permit or sponsorship guarantee.

Fields. Concatenated title + employer + detail description. Location, occupation category, and URLs are not scanned.

Threshold. Exact hits add their lexicon weight. Score ≥ FOREIGN_SCORE_THRESHOLD = 2 is required to appear. Ads below 2 are fetched and inspected, then omitted from the board.

Counting. Each lexicon string is matched once (str.find on NFC-normalized, casefolded text). Repeating the same string does not add weight again. Distinct inflections are separate entries and can both count (e.g. radna dozvola and radnu dozvolu). A longer hit can also fire a shorter entry that is a substring of it.

Negation. A hit is discarded if any marker appears in the 25 characters immediately before it (NEGATION_WINDOW_CHARS = 25 in scoring.py): ne , nije , nema , bez , isključivo eu, samo eu državljani. Markers after the phrase are not checked. Examples that drop: “nema radne dozvole”, “samo EU državljani”.

Fuzzy fallback. If exact matching totals 0 and rapidfuzz is installed, near-inflections (partial_ratio ≥ 90) each add FUZZY_WEIGHT 1. Fuzzy hits skip the negation window. A single fuzzy hit is not enough.

Weight 3 — permit and third-country legal wording

One hit includes the ad (3 ≥ 2). These are statutory labels for residence-and-work permits, work permits, obtaining a permit, and third-country nationals. They are a keep signal because HZZ copy uses them when the vacancy is aimed at people who need a Croatian permit — not EU free movement.

Weight 2 — foreign-national and sponsorship language

One hit includes the ad (2 ≥ 2). Weight is 2, not 3, because the wording is not third-country-specific: strani državljani can mean any non-Croatian national, including EU. Unlike weight 1, it does not need a second hit to reach the threshold.

Weight 1 — weak corroboration

One hit does not include the ad (1 < 2). Accommodation and field-work phrasing often co-occur with non-local hiring, but also appear on domestic and English-language jobs. They count only combined with another weight-1 phrase or a weight-2/3 phrase.

Retention

Dated ads: 3 calendar days after deadline_date. Open-ended ads: 90 days after first seen.

Refresh

GitHub Actions daily at 06:00 UTC, plus a manual full scrape. generated_at on jobs.json is the last successful export.

Identifiers

IDs are HZZ WebSifra values.

Metoda

Podskup javnih oglasa HZZ Burze rada. Nije cijeli katalog. Nije jamstvo radne dozvole ni sponzorstva.

Uključivanje

Oglas se prikazuje samo ako vrijedi sve sljedeće:

  1. Županija. Grad Zagreb, radio vrijednost 4. Kategorije zanimanja se prolaze pojedinačno; „Svi poslovi” ima gornju granicu od 300 redaka.
  2. Lokacija. location_score > 0 (2 = centar grada, 1 = Zagreb, 0 = odbacivanje).
  3. Strani rezultat. Ponderirane fraze u naslovu + poslodavcu + opisu. Prikazuje se samo ako je rezultat ≥ FOREIGN_SCORE_THRESHOLD (2). Detalji niže.

Druge županije se ne prikupljaju. Pregledani ne-pogoci se ne prikazuju.

Strani rezultat

Jedini filter uz prikupljanje oglasa HZZ-a za Grad Zagreb. To je ponderirani rezultat podnizova nad javnim tekstom oglasa. Nije jamstvo radne dozvole ni sponzorstva.

Polja. Spojeni title + employer + opis s detaljne stranice (description). Lokacija, kategorija zanimanja i URL-ovi se ne pregledavaju.

Prag. Točni pogoci zbrajaju težinu iz leksikona. Za prikaz treba rezultat ≥ FOREIGN_SCORE_THRESHOLD = 2. Oglasi ispod 2 se dohvaćaju i pregledavaju, zatim se izostavljaju s ploče.

Brojanje. Svaki niz iz leksikona se poklapa jednom (str.find na NFC-normaliziranom, casefold tekstu). Ponavljanje istog niza ne dodaje težinu ponovno. Različiti padeži su zasebni unosi i mogu se oba zbrojiti (npr. radna dozvola i radnu dozvolu). Dulji pogodak može aktivirati i kraći unos koji je njegov podniz.

Negacija. Pogodak se odbacuje ako se bilo koja oznaka pojavi u 25 znakova neposredno prije njega (NEGATION_WINDOW_CHARS = 25 u scoring.py): ne , nije , nema , bez , isključivo eu, samo eu državljani. Oznake nakon fraze se ne provjeravaju. Primjeri koji padaju: „nema radne dozvole”, „samo EU državljani”.

Fuzzy rezervni prolaz. Ako točno poklapanje zbroji 0 i rapidfuzz je instaliran, bliske infleksije (partial_ratio ≥ 90) svaka dodaju FUZZY_WEIGHT 1. Fuzzy pogoci ne prolaze prozor negacije. Jedan fuzzy pogodak nije dovoljan.
